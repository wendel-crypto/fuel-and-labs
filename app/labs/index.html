What the markers mean

ApoB counts the particles that carry cholesterol into artery walls; LDL is the cholesterol they carry. Lp(a) is a genetic particle. hs-CRP measures inflammation. TSH is the brain's signal to the thyroid. eGFR estimates kidney filtration from creatinine, which runs high in muscular people; cystatin C ignores muscle. PTH controls calcium. Ferritin is stored iron. Fructosamine reflects blood sugar over 2–3 weeks. GGT is a liver enzyme that rises with alcohol and liver fat. Educational only — review with your doctor. Get checked promptly for chest pain on exertion, fainting, unusual breathlessness or palpitations.
